# Q890 primary-source comparison

Checked 2026-11-27. High confidence in the exact model and source match. No later scope-matching algorithm was found in the bounded primary-source search. Publication priority remains unestablished.

- Baier, Piribauer and Ziemek, *Foundations of probability-raising causality in Markov decision processes*, LMCS 20(1), 2024, DOI 10.46298/LMCS-20(1:4)2024. Definition 6.11 (printed p. 4:57) defines a co-safety SPR cause by (coS), strict probability raising on each minimal good prefix under every scheduler giving it positive probability. Section 6.2.1 assumes a supplied DFA for exactly those minimal good prefixes and an explicit DRA for the regular effect. The open verification question appears after Example 6.14 (p. 4:60). There is no separately labelled (coM) in Definition 6.11. Earlier conditions for state-set causes and later optional temporal-priority restrictions concern other tasks. The same paper treats the Markov-chain special case in polynomial time. [Published definition](https://lmcs.episciences.org/12897/pdf#page=57) · [Exact open passage](https://lmcs.episciences.org/12897/pdf#page=60)
- Lemma 6.12 gives a reduction for co-safety global PR on the whole cause event. Lemma 6.13 transfers coS to state-set SPR in only one direction. Example 6.14 shows that merging histories can lose a zero-gap word even with a reachability effect. Ziemek's *Probabilistic Causality in Markovian Models*, §5.3, Table 5.2 (printed p. 139), Definition 99 and Remark 104, still list coS verification as unresolved. The author arXiv record shows v6, dated January 18, 2024, as the last version. [Thesis](https://tud.qucosa.de/api/qucosa%3A93413/attachment/ATT-0/) · [Author record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02973)
- The earlier “local PR” framework in Baier et al., *From Verification to Causality-Based Explications* (ICALP 2021), treats a fixed Markov chain and a distinct minimality condition. State-based SPR checks and trace-p-cause algorithms likewise use different input models or quantifiers. Their results do not establish the explicit word-by-word comparison over all schedulers. [ICALP invited paper](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2021.1) · [Trace-p-cause paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11334-022-00452-8)

(AAAI 2025), Definition 7 and §4, study state-set predictors and the proportion of memoryless policies satisfying a PR test. Oura et al. (UAI 2026), §§2–3, characterize reachability causes for singleton states and learn them in unknown MDPs. Oura and Ito (UAI 2025) investigate uncertain parametric reachability cases. None gives the universal full-word coS check. [AAAI proceedings](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/34879/37034) · [UAI 2026 author version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29681) · [UAI 2025 proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v286/oura25a.html)

Maximal winning end components reduce the latter two value computations to reachability. The new step is to encode first deviation from one word via a nonnegative payoff and decide whether relaxed equality is attained on a path with positive continuation. [Technical report](https://digicoll.lib.berkeley.edu/record/135325/files/EECS-2007-122.pdf)
