Why the relaxed optimum can tie

The verifier pays 1 on completing the cause word. On deviating it adds the deviation state's maximum effect chance and subtracts the effect chance from t. Action A never visits t. If it is the only maximizing action when the relaxed optimum is one, its equality does not furnish a violating word.
