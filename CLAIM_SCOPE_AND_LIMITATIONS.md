# Scope and limitations

The theorem concerns the strict condition (coS) of Definition 6.11. A supplied DFA is assumed to accept exactly the minimal good prefixes of the nonempty co-safety candidate. The nonempty regular effect has an explicit deterministic Rabin automaton. Both automata read physical MDP states. A candidate with no feasible prefix satisfies the quantified condition vacuously. A minimal prefix is tested only when its cylinder has positive mass under the scheduler.

The theorem permits arbitrary history-dependent randomized schedulers under full state observation. General global PR, cause synthesis, compact temporal logic inputs and hidden observations lie outside this theorem. Conditions imposed in other causality frameworks are separate from Definition 6.11.

The rational replay tests finite families. External peer review, formal verification and an exhaustive priority search remain pending.
